- Convert pictures, videos and PDFs from the right-click menu.
- Copy or move the selection to a pinned or recent folder.
- Compress to a name you choose; a single file's zip is named without its extension.
- A Network section in the sidebar: open a server to browse all its shares, like \\server on Windows, and pick an icon for a server or disk.
- Open iCloud Drive and sync folders, with the Finder's cloud icons.
- Choose whether Home shows recent files, and Kamiro has its own icon: two glass folders on blue.
